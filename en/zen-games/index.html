About the game

Zen Games brings four puzzles together in a calm Japanese garden. Pick a game from the menu and keep the same nickname, trophies and daily streak across all four.

Zen Blocks: place blocks on the grid and clear lines. Zen Match: swap flowers to line up three, across 100 levels in 10 worlds. Zen Tea: pour teas from tube to tube to sort the colours. Zen Merge: merge pebbles up to 2048 and beyond.

  • Zen Blocks · block puzzle
  • Zen Match · 100 match-3 levels
  • Zen Tea · colour sorting
  • Zen Merge · pebble merging

How to play

  1. Choose your nickname the first time you play.
  2. Pick a game from the garden menu.
  3. Take on the daily challenge to earn flowers.
  4. Climb each game's online leaderboard.
