Zen Games: rules and tips for all 4 zen puzzles

Zen Games brings four relaxing puzzles together in one garden. You keep the same nickname, daily streak and rewards across all four. Here are the rules and our best tips.

Zen Games

Play now

Zen Blocks

Drag the blocks from the bottom onto the grid. A full row or column blooms and disappears.

  • Clearing several lines at once gives a combo: more points.
  • When the grid is completely empty, you move to the next level with new colours.
  • Always keep room for the big L or bar piece: it is the one that blocks you most often.

Zen Match

Swipe a flower towards its neighbour to line up at least 3 identical flowers. The level goal and the moves left are shown at the top.

  • Line up 4 or 5 flowers to create a bonus: stripe, bomb or joker.
  • The 100 levels are spread across 10 worlds, with ice to break and caged flowers.
  • Stuck? The 3 buttons at the bottom help: hammer, shuffle and extra moves.
  • Play near the bottom of the grid first: falling cascades often create free combos.

Zen Tea

Tap a tube, then another one, to pour the top tea. You can only pour onto the same colour or into an empty tube. You win when every tube holds a single colour.

  • The fewer moves you make, the more stars you earn.
  • Keep an empty tube as long as you can: it is your joker.
  • An extra tube or a hint can be offered when you are stuck.

Zen Merge

Slide the whole board (by swiping or with the arrow keys). Two identical pebbles that touch merge: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, and so on.

  • Keep your biggest pebble in a corner and never move it.
  • Mostly use two directions, for example left and down, to keep the board tidy.
  • Aim for 512, then 2048!

The garden, missions and streak

Daily missions earn you flowers to decorate your garden. Come back every day to grow your streak: a special gift waits for you on days 3 and 7.
