Tips · Sainte-Agathe : Hôpital Hanté

Sainte-Agathe: tips to survive and escape the haunted hospital

The game itself is in French, so in-game labels are given in French with a translation. Sainte-Agathe is a horror game where you will die a lot, especially at first. Here is everything you need to find the 5 fuses, understand each monster and get out alive through the courtyard gate.

Sainte-Agathe : Hôpital Hanté

Play now

1. Your goal for the night

  1. Find the 5 glowing yellow fuses (fusibles). They are hidden in rooms, upstairs and in the basement, and move every night.
  2. Bring them to the GÉNÉRATEUR (generator) room, on the main corridor opposite the Chapelle (chapel). While you carry a fuse, a ⚡ shows the way.
  3. With all 5 fuses in place, power comes back and the courtyard gate opens. Run to the far east end of the main corridor.

You can drop the fuses off one at a time. But every fuse you pick up makes noise and wakes new monsters.

2. The monsters and how to lose them

  • Le Veilleur (the Watcher): huge, sees far. When he spots you he stops and stares for a second. That is your cue to run: break his line of sight, then hide in a locker (casier), but never in front of him.
  • La Rampante (the Crawler): blind but hears everything. Never run near her, walk. In a locker, hold your breath (Space) as she passes.
  • Le Pantin (the Puppet): only moves when you are not looking at him. Keep him in view and back away slowly. Your flashlight helps: in the dark you cannot see him.
  • La Chirurgienne (the Surgeon): patrols the 2nd floor. She wakes after your 2nd fuse, or after a few minutes.

Monsters do not chase you forever: break their line of sight long enough and they give up. Many rooms are connected: run in loops.

3. The blackout (coupure) and the Prowler

When the siren sounds, a 20-second countdown appears and the lights turn red. At zero everything goes dark and the Prowler (le Rôdeur) races through the corridors, taking anyone it sees.

  • Safest: get into a locker before the countdown ends.
  • Otherwise: the back of a room, far from the door, out of sight.
  • The entrance hall is always a safe zone.

4. The basement and Room 0 (Salle 0)

The basement stairs are at the west end of the service corridor. There is no map down there. One fuse is always in Room 0, which is locked: find the rusty key (clé rouillée) hidden in the basement first. When you take that fuse, the Watcher bursts out of the crematorium: get back upstairs fast.

Never stay in total darkness in the basement (flashlight off, far from bulbs): the Shadow (l'Ombre) comes for you. Turn your light back on and it vanishes.

5. Your tools

  • Flashlight (F): recharges while switched off.
  • Camcorder (V): night vision. Its battery only refills with green batteries (piles).
  • Flash (R or right click): blinds the Watcher and the Surgeon, freezes the Puppet. Useless against the blind Crawler.
  • One fuse is a trap: it glows a little more orange. Take it and it screams, drawing the monsters to you.

6. Which role to pick?

  • The Nurse (Infirmière) runs 60% longer: best for escaping.
  • The Electrician (Électricien) has a flashlight that lasts twice as long: best for the basement.
  • The Investigator (Enquêteur) sees fuses and notes through walls: best for a first game.
