Pulse Forge: the guide to building the richest mine

Pulse Forge looks simple: tap the miner and money pours in. But the mine only runs fast when the whole chain keeps up, and the rock always ends up pushing. Here is how to progress without wasting money or losing a tunnel.

Pulse Forge

Play now

1. The chain: miner, elevator, truck

Ore always follows the same path. Miners cut it and fill their tunnel's cart. The elevator picks it up and brings it to the depot. The truck sells it at the surface. Your income per second is the speed of the slowest link: a level 50 tunnel is useless if the elevator is level 5.

  • The game flags the “bottleneck” at the surface: upgrade it first.
  • If the depot overflows, selling (the truck) is the problem. If carts stay full, it is the elevator.
  • The 2nd elevator cage doubles the flow to the surface: buy it once your deep tunnels produce a lot.

2. Bosses: a mine that runs without you

At first you must tap every link by hand. The first real milestone is hiring a boss for the coal tunnel, the elevator and selling: then everything runs on its own, even with the game closed. Your bosses keep the mine going while you are away and you collect the earnings when you return.

For each new tunnel the boss is expensive at first, but pays off fast. A tunnel without a boss only works when you tap it.

3. ×2 milestones every 25 levels

Each tunnel doubles its output at levels 25, 50, 75, 100… The small “×2 in …” counter under the level tells you how many upgrades are left. When you are a few levels away from a milestone, it is almost always the best buy.

  • Extra miners (up to 4 per tunnel) multiply the tunnel's output.
  • The drill gives a big bonus but costs a lot: save it for your best tunnels.
  • Opening a new tunnel is often worth more than 20 levels on the old one: each seam pays far more than the last.

4. Pressure and props

The more a tunnel works, the more the rock pushes. The coloured bar under each tunnel goes from green to red. At 100% the tunnel collapses: it stops and must be cleared. Set props to bring the pressure down before it reaches red.

  • Watch your deep tunnels most: they earn the most, so they are the ones you cannot afford to lose.
  • The Timberman, first foreman (from 2 tunnels), re-props every tunnel at once.
  • The Hewer (4 tunnels) gives ×3 for 20 seconds, the Hauler (6 tunnels) empties every cart.

5. What changes every day

  • The daily seam: one tunnel produces ×3, but the rock pushes 2.5 times harder there. Profitable if you keep propping it.
  • Orders: three a day, paid in ingots. Deliver them: ingots are the most valuable resource in the game.
  • At dawn an event sometimes awaits (cave-in, rich vein, flooding…) with two choices: read the effects before deciding.
  • Coming back several days in a row raises your streak and the daily bonus.

6. Rush shift

The Rush shift button doubles all production for 30 seconds, then recharges. Use it just before a big purchase. The ×2 for 4 hours bonus, offered for an ad, pays best if you leave the mine running for a long time.

7. Selling the mine and the retreat

Selling the mine restarts you from zero, but you keep your ingots, and each one adds +2% production forever. Do it when progress clearly slows down: the second run goes much faster.

When the mine hits bottom, the retreat unlocks: you pull out your own props starting from the bottom, each one releases a fortune and brings the tunnel down. The collapse front rises on its own: move up tunnel by tunnel without getting caught. The ingots you earn buy permanent perks in the Shop.

8. Recommended order

  1. Upgrade coal until you can open copper.
  2. Hire the coal, elevator and selling bosses.
  3. Aim for ×2 milestones (level 25) and open tunnels one by one.
  4. Keep an eye on pressure and the bottleneck.
  5. Deliver daily orders and stack ingots.
  6. Sell the mine when progress slows, then start the retreat.

Install the Pixel Pulse app

Put every game on your home screen: they open full screen, like a real app. Free, no account, very light.

✅ Installed! Find Pixel Pulse on your home screen.

Your browser can install the app in one click.

Open in Chrome
  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. The Pixel Pulse icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to play.

Samsung Internet works too: ☰ menu → Add page to → Home screen.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. Done!

On iPhone, Chrome works too (iOS 16.4 or later): Share button on the right of the address bar.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon (a small screen with an arrow) on the right of the address bar.
  3. Or: ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app (Edge: … → Apps).
  4. Click Install: Pixel Pulse opens in its own window.

Firefox and Safari on Mac cannot install websites yet: use Chrome or Edge.
