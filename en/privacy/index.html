Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Pixel Pulse aims to collect as little data as possible. This page explains what the site and games store, and why.

Your saved games

Your progress (money, levels, settings) and the list of your latest games are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are not sent to our servers, and you can erase them by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Online leaderboards

If you choose a nickname, that nickname and your score are sent to our database (Supabase, hosted in the European Union) to show the leaderboards. Do not use your real name or personal information as a nickname. To have an entry removed, write to us.

Advertising

The site shows Google AdSense ads, and some games offer to watch an ad in exchange for a bonus. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure these ads. Where the law requires it, your consent is asked before any advertising cookie, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages service), 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, github.com. The host keeps technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) for security and to keep the service running.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete data about you by writing to:

contact@playpixelpulsing.fr

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).
