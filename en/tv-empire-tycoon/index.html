About the game

You start with a TV News show already on air, earning from the very first second. Then open the Weather, Sport, Talk Show, Cinema, Game Show and Concert studios: one click on a card places the studio in your building.

Add floors, dig basements, buy upgrades like the Canteen, the Control Room or FM Radio, and grow the city around your channel to win more viewers. Goals guide you step by step, and your channel keeps earning even while you are away.

  • 7 studios to open
  • Floors, basements and a city
  • Goals with rewards
  • Offline earnings

How to play

  1. Press DIFFUSER (broadcast) to air your first shows.
  2. Buy a new studio with one click on its card.
  3. Expand your building with floors and basements.
  4. Complete goals to earn bonuses.
