Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Pixel Pulse aims to collect as little data as possible. This page explains what the site and games store, and why.

Your saved games

Your progress (money, levels, settings) and the list of your latest games are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are not sent to our servers, and you can erase them by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Online leaderboards

If you choose a nickname, that nickname and your score are sent to our database (Supabase, hosted in the European Union) to show the leaderboards. Do not use your real name or personal information as a nickname. To have an entry removed, write to us.

Game ratings

When you rate a game, your rating (1 to 5 stars) is stored with a random anonymous ID kept in your browser, so that only one rating counts per device. Nothing else is sent (Supabase, hosted in the European Union).

Advertising

The site shows Google AdSense ads, and some games offer to watch an ad in exchange for a bonus. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure these ads. Where the law requires it, your consent is asked before any advertising cookie, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more: how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a Google-certified consent window appears on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time with the “Privacy” link shown at the bottom of the screen.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages service), 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, github.com. The host keeps technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) for security and to keep the service running.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete data about you by writing to:

contact@playpixelpulsing.fr

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Install the app

Install the Pixel Pulse app

Put every game on your home screen: they open full screen, like a real app. Free, no account, very light.

✅ Installed! Find Pixel Pulse on your home screen.

Your browser can install the app in one click.

Open in Chrome
  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. The Pixel Pulse icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to play.

Samsung Internet works too: ☰ menu → Add page to → Home screen.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. Done!

On iPhone, Chrome works too (iOS 16.4 or later): Share button on the right of the address bar.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon (a small screen with an arrow) on the right of the address bar.
  3. Or: ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app (Edge: … → Apps).
  4. Click Install: Pixel Pulse opens in its own window.

Firefox and Safari on Mac cannot install websites yet: use Chrome or Edge.
