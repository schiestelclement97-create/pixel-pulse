Frequently asked questions

Are Pixel Pulse games free?

Yes, every game is free. Some offer to watch an ad to get a bonus or continue a game: it is always your choice.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. The games run right in your browser, with no sign-up. On a phone you can also add them to your home screen and open them like an app.

Do they work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The games are made for touch screens as well as mouse and keyboard: Android, iPhone, tablets and computers.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, your game is saved in your browser, on your device. If you clear the site's data or switch devices, your progress starts over.

How do I appear on a leaderboard?

Pick a nickname in the game, no account needed. Your best score is sent to the online leaderboard with that nickname.
