About the game

You start with a TV News show already on air, earning from the very first second. Then open the Weather, Sport, Talk Show, Cinema, Game Show and Concert studios: one click on a card places the studio in your building.

Add floors, dig basements, buy upgrades like the Canteen, the Control Room or FM Radio, and grow the city around your channel to win more viewers. Goals guide you step by step, and your channel keeps earning even while you are away.

  • 7 studios to open
  • Floors, basements and a city
  • Goals with rewards
  • Offline earnings

Need help? Our tips to get started and go further in TV Empire Tycoon.

Read the tips

How to play

  1. Press DIFFUSER (broadcast) to air your first shows.
  2. Buy a new studio with one click on its card.
  3. Expand your building with floors and basements.
  4. Complete goals to earn bonuses.

Controls

Computer

Click DIFFUSER (broadcast) to start your shows and click the cards to buy studios and upgrades.

Phone

Tap DIFFUSER (broadcast) and the cards. The game is in French. The screen is laid out in three clear areas: your channel, your building and your purchases.

A word from the creator

TV Empire Tycoon was rebuilt from scratch for the web, with a cartoon style and a screen split into three areas so it stays readable even on a small phone.

Questions about TV Empire Tycoon

Does my channel earn while I'm away?

Yes, your studios keep earning offline, and you collect when you return.

Which studio should I buy first?

Follow the objectives: they show the next useful step and give a bonus each time.

What are floors and basements for?

They make room for new studios and upgrades, so more viewers.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically in your browser, on this device.

Install the app

Install the Pixel Pulse app

Put every game on your home screen: they open full screen, like a real app. Free, no account, very light.

✅ Installed! Find Pixel Pulse on your home screen.

Your browser can install the app in one click.

Open in Chrome
  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. The Pixel Pulse icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to play.

Samsung Internet works too: ☰ menu → Add page to → Home screen.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. Done!

On iPhone, Chrome works too (iOS 16.4 or later): Share button on the right of the address bar.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon (a small screen with an arrow) on the right of the address bar.
  3. Or: ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app (Edge: … → Apps).
  4. Click Install: Pixel Pulse opens in its own window.

Firefox and Safari on Mac cannot install websites yet: use Chrome or Edge.
