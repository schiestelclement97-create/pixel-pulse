About the game

November 2026. On a dare, you sneak into Sainte-Agathe psychiatric hospital, closed in 1987 after 43 patients vanished. The door slams shut behind you. By itself. The only way out: the courtyard gate, locked by electricity.

Search a huge hospital over four levels (ground floor, psychiatric wing, operating block and a flooded basement), find the 5 hidden fuses and bring the power back. But the Watcher, the Crawler, the Puppet and the Surgeon wake up one by one. Hide in lockers, keep your flashlight charged and survive the blackouts. The game itself is in French.

  • First-person horror
  • 4 monsters with different rules
  • Hospital over 4 levels
  • Flashlight, camcorder and lockers

How to play

  1. Pick your role: Nurse, Electrician or Investigator.
  2. Search the rooms and grab the 5 fuses (E key).
  3. Hide in a locker (casier) when a monster gets close or the siren sounds.
  4. Put the fuses back in the generator, then run out through the courtyard gate.
