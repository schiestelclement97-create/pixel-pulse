About the game

In Pulse Studio Tycoon you pick your creator and start with a small studio. Shoot videos, cash in and reinvest to open Studio 2, the Live Cooking, Live Music and Live Orbit sets, then the cafeteria and staff room, all in 3D.

Each studio levels up to hold more gear: phones, mics, ring lights, cameras, mixing desks. Viral Buzz multiplies your income for a few seconds, collabs give you a boost, and your subscribers push you up the world leaderboard.

  • 3D studios and city
  • TV news show with guests
  • Sponsors, buzz and collabs
  • Online world leaderboard

How to play

  1. Pick your creator and nickname.
  2. Press Tourner (shoot) to earn your first euros.
  3. Buy gear and level up your studios.
  4. Open new sets and climb the leaderboard.

Controls

Computer

Click 🎬 Film to earn your first euros, then the purchase buttons to equip your studios and open new ones.

Phone

Tap 🎬 Film and the purchase buttons: everything is played by touch.

Questions about Pulse Studio Tycoon

How do I appear on the world leaderboard?

Choose a nickname on first launch: your subscribers are sent to the leaderboard, and you climb as your channel grows.

How do I unlock the News show?

Open Studio 2, then launch the News: guests arrive by car and you just tap their bubble to do the interview.

Can I continue my game on another device?

Yes, with the online save by code in the options. Write your code down.

Is the game in English?

Yes, since version 3.0: the game opens in the language of the page you came from, and you can change it in the options.

Install the app

Install the Pixel Pulse app

Put every game on your home screen: they open full screen, like a real app. Free, no account, very light.

✅ Installed! Find Pixel Pulse on your home screen.

Your browser can install the app in one click.

Open in Chrome
  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. The Pixel Pulse icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to play.

Samsung Internet works too: ☰ menu → Add page to → Home screen.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. Done!

On iPhone, Chrome works too (iOS 16.4 or later): Share button on the right of the address bar.

  1. Open playpixelpulsing.fr in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon (a small screen with an arrow) on the right of the address bar.
  3. Or: ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app (Edge: … → Apps).
  4. Click Install: Pixel Pulse opens in its own window.

Firefox and Safari on Mac cannot install websites yet: use Chrome or Edge.
